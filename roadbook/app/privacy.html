Privacy Notice

Last updated: [date]

This notice explains how [Organisation name] uses personal data in Roadbook, in line with Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020. In short: your records live on your phone first, and are copied to your account only if you make one.

1. Who we are

[Organisation name], [address], is responsible for your personal data in Roadbook. Privacy questions go to [contact name] at [privacy email].

2. Using Roadbook without an account

You can use Roadbook with no account. Everything you enter stays on your phone and is never sent to us. If you clear the app's data or lose the phone, those records are gone, so make a backup file from Settings.

3. What we collect when you make an account

  • Your name, email address and a password (the password is handled by our sign-in provider and we cannot read it)
  • Your truck name or number and your preferred currency
  • Your records: expenses, income, receipt photos, trips (distance, start and end time, and the route your phone recorded), loads, delivery proofs, reminders and planned routes
  • For deliveries: the time, the place (GPS), the receiver's name, a signature and a photo if you add them, and a digital fingerprint of the proof
  • If you join a crew: your chat messages and road alerts (including the place you reported), shared with that crew

Roadbook does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Your sign-in (email and password) is part of one account system shared by [Organisation name]'s apps, so the same login may exist for another of our apps; Roadbook records are kept separately and are not visible to those apps.

4. Why we use it

To keep your records safe and available on your devices, to let your fleet owner or dispatcher send you loads and see delivery proof, to let your crew chat and warn each other about the road, and to keep accounts secure. We do not sell your data and do not use it for anything else without telling you first.

5. Who can see it

  • Only you can see your expenses, income, trips, reminders and routes, unless you choose to share with a fleet owner.
  • Your company's owner and admins (dispatchers) can see your trips, loads, expenses, income and receipt photos only if you switch on "Share my totals with the owner" when joining a crew (your reminders and planned routes stay private). You can turn this off in Crew settings at any time. A person who sends you a load can always see that load and its delivery proof (time, place, receiver, signature and photo), because they need it to confirm the delivery, even if you stop sharing or leave the crew.
  • Companies are separate. Each company (crew) only ever sees its own people, loads, chat and alerts. Another company cannot see yours.
  • Your crew sees your chat messages and road alerts, with your name.
  • Our service providers process data for us under written agreements: [Supabase for the database, sign-in and file storage; your web host].

6. Things that leave your phone only when you tap them

  • Voice typing. When you tap the microphone, your phone's browser may send your voice to its speech service (on Chrome this is run by Google). Reading alerts aloud happens on the phone itself.
  • Maps, Waze and WhatsApp. When you tap those buttons, the place or message you chose is handed to that app.
  • Receipt scanning happens on your phone. Receipt photos are not sent anywhere except to your own account storage, and location details are removed from the photo first.

7. Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase in [region / country]. Where data is handled outside Jamaica, we rely on [safeguard, such as the provider's data processing agreement] so it stays protected.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your records until you delete them or delete your Roadbook data. Deleting your Roadbook data (Settings, then "Delete all my Roadbook data") removes your Roadbook records, files and crew messages straight away. Because the sign-in is shared with our other apps, the login itself is removed only if you also ask us to at [privacy email]. A record you delete inside the app is hidden from you and your devices, and is removed for good when you delete your Roadbook data[or after your chosen clean-up period]. Phone copies can be erased in Settings.

9. How we protect it

  • Connections are encrypted, and data is encrypted when stored by our provider
  • Every table has row-level security, so each person can reach only the rows they are allowed to
  • Files are kept in a private store, not on public links
  • Strict browser security rules block unknown scripts

If personal data is ever lost or exposed in a way that could harm you, we will tell you and the Information Commissioner as the law requires.

10. Your rights

You can see, correct and delete most of your data yourself in the app, and download all of it from Settings. For anything else, ask at [privacy email] and we will reply within the time the law allows. You may object to how your data is used or ask us to restrict it.

11. Children

Roadbook is for working drivers and is not meant for people under 18.

12. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can put things right. You may also complain to the Office of the Information Commissioner of Jamaica.

13. Changes to this notice

If we change how we use data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Have this notice reviewed by legal counsel before you publish it.
