Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: [date]

[Organisation name] wants Roadbook to be easy for every driver, including people with poor eyesight, hearing loss, limited hand movement, or who use screen readers.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we have done

  • Large buttons and text built for use in a truck cab, with a text-size setting up to "huge"
  • Light and night looks, with colour contrast checked for both
  • Alerts are shown, spoken aloud and vibrate, so no single sense is needed
  • Everything can be used with a keyboard or a screen reader; fields have labels and errors are written in words
  • Icons always come with words, and important information never relies on colour alone
  • Plain, short wording, and a Help screen with step-by-step guides

Last tested on [date] with an automated accessibility checker (axe) on every screen, in day and night looks and at the largest text size, plus keyboard-only checks. We have not yet run a full test with every screen reader.

Known limitations

  • Voice typing and spoken alerts depend on the speech features of your phone's browser, which vary between phones.
  • Route sketches and trip maps are simple drawings. The distance and places are always also given as text.
  • Photos you take (receipts, delivery proof) cannot be described to a screen reader automatically.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, email [accessibility email] with the screen, what happened, and the phone or tool you were using. We will reply within [x] working days.
